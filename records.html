Timeline of impossibles

  1. 2012

    91 goals in a calendar year — FIFA-certified all-competition record.

  2. 2015

    Only player to win two European Trebles (2009 & 2015) with the same club.

  3. 2019

    Most La Liga hat-tricks (36) and assists in a single season (22).

  4. 2021

    First South American man to win Copa América Golden Boot + Golden Ball in the same tournament.

  5. 2022

    First player to win two World Cup Golden Balls, delivering 7 goals & 3 assists in Qatar.
